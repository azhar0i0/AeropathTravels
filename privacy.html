Who we are

Arrowpak Travels (“Arrowpak”, “we”, “us”) is a software house in Lahore, Pakistan. We build and support back-office software, booking websites, SEO, answer engine optimisation (AEO) and social media services for travel companies.

For questions about this policy or your information, email hello@arrowpaktravels.com.

What this policy covers

This policy applies to:

  • Visitors to arrowpaktravels.com and its pages.
  • People who contact us by the website form, email, phone or WhatsApp, including prospective clients.
  • Our clients’ staff who use systems we build or host, and the passengers, customers and employees whose records those systems hold.

Our clients’ own websites have their own privacy policies. Where we operate a system on a client’s behalf, that client decides how its customers’ data is used, and we follow its instructions (see section 05).

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Your name, company, email address and phone number.
  • The services you are interested in and anything you write in your message.
  • Details shared during calls, meetings and the project itself, such as how your team works today.
  • Billing details needed to invoice your company.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data such as IP address, browser, device type, pages viewed and the time of each visit, recorded in server logs.
  • Usage data from analytics tools, if they are enabled on this site (see section 09).

Information from clients

When we build, migrate or host a system for a client, we may handle records the client provides, such as passenger names, passport and visa details, booking and payment records, and employee and payroll information.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a call.
  • To prepare a written scope and quote.
  • To deliver, support and improve the services in your contract.
  • To send invoices and keep financial records.
  • To keep our website and systems secure and to prevent misuse.
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use client data to train AI models or for our own marketing.

If you are in the UK or the European Union, we rely on these legal bases: your consent (for example, analytics cookies), performance of a contract, our legitimate interest in running and protecting our business, and compliance with legal obligations.

Data in client systems

For the records held inside a system we build or host, the client controls that data and we process it on their behalf. In that role we:

  • Use the data only to build, run, support and back up the system, as the client instructs.
  • Limit access to the team members who need it for the work, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Tell the client without undue delay if we become aware of a security incident affecting its data.
  • Return or delete the data when the contract ends, unless the law requires us to keep it.

If you are a customer or employee of one of our clients and want to access or correct your information, please contact that company first. We will help them respond.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only when needed, and only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate, such as hosting, email, cloud storage, analytics and payment providers, bound by confidentiality and data protection terms.
  • Advertising and social platforms, only when a client asks us to run campaigns on its behalf, using the client’s own accounts.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect our rights, our clients or the public.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is reorganised or sold, under the same protections described here.

Some providers store data outside Pakistan. When that happens, we choose providers with recognised security standards and appropriate contractual safeguards.

How long we keep it

Enquiries
Up to 24 months after our last contact, if no project follows.
Client and project records
For the length of the contract, then as long as tax and accounting law requires.
Data in client systems
As instructed by the client, then returned or deleted at the end of the contract.
Server logs
Up to 90 days, unless needed to investigate a security issue.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls with individual accounts, regular backups and software updates to protect the information we hold. Access to client systems is limited to named team members and reviewed when people change roles.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We work to fix any issue we find and will notify affected clients where required.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses only what it needs to work. If we add analytics to understand which pages are useful, we will use privacy-respecting settings and ask for consent where the law requires it. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will still work.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete your information when we no longer need it.
  • Stop or limit certain uses, or withdraw consent you have given.
  • Send your information to another provider in a portable format.

Email hello@arrowpaktravels.com with your request. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we aim to reply within 30 days. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can also contact your local data protection authority.

Our website and services are meant for businesses and are not directed at children under 16.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will tell clients directly about significant changes.
