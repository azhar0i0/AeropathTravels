    Ask us directly
    How much does a system or website cost?

    It depends on the modules you need: invoicing, accounting, payroll, bookings, a public website or all of them. After a discovery call we send a written scope with a fixed quote, so the price does not change unless the scope does.

    How long does a project take?

    A website usually goes live within a few weeks. A back office is rolled out one module at a time, so your team starts using the first part, often invoicing or bookings, while the next one is built. The timeline is written into your scope.

    Do you work with travel companies outside Pakistan?

    Yes. We build and run websites for agencies in the USA as well as Pakistan, including Qibla Gateway and Globe Link Tours. Calls happen on Google Meet or Zoom at a time that suits your time zone.

    Can you move our data from spreadsheets or an old system?

    Yes. We import customers, bookings, invoices, supplier balances and opening ledger figures, check the totals with your accounts team, and only switch over once the numbers match.

    Who hosts the software, and who owns it?

    We can host and maintain it for you, or deploy it to your own server. Either way your data and your domain stay yours, and you can ask for a full export at any time.

    What support do we get after launch?

    Support messages are answered the same working day. A monthly plan covers hosting, backups, updates and small changes, and larger additions are quoted separately.

    Can you do SEO, AEO or social media for a site you did not build?

    Yes. We start with an audit of your current site and accounts, then work on package pages, FAQs, Google Business Profile and a monthly content calendar, with a report that shows which pages and posts bring in enquiries.

    Can we meet you in person?

    Yes. Our main office is in Sector H Commercial, DHA Phase 1, Lahore, and our second office is in DHA Phase 2. Email us or send the form first so we can book a time at either office and have the right people there.
